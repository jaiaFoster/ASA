## Adversarial review

Explicitly answer:

- Has the result been independently replicated?
- Have replications failed?
- Does later research reduce the original claim?
- Did the effect persist after publication?
- Could data mining, survivorship, or selection bias explain it?
- Is performance concentrated in a small period?
- Are results parameter-dependent?
- Could realistic transaction costs change the conclusion?
- Are execution assumptions realistic?
- Is the effect actually another known factor exposure?
- Does it require unavailable-at-decision-time information?
- Where is it known to fail?
- Is there evidence of crowding or decay?
- What evidence would make this dossier misleading if omitted?
