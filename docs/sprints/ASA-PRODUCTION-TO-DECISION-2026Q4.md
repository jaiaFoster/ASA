**Purpose:** Define the relationship among the five new sprint prompts and the still-open ASA-OPTIONS-TO-OUTCOMES-2026Q4 program so there is one canonical owner for each existing observation, ledger, and closure obligation.

## 1. Five-phase sequence

1. PRODUCTION-TRUST-001
2. SYSTEM-UNDERSTANDING-001
3. TRADE-TRUST-001
4. OUTCOME-VALUE-001
5. SCALE-READINESS-001

The sequence is dependency-ordered but may overlap in calendar time when an earlier sprint is implementation-complete and only observation remains. A failed observation reopens the owning earlier boundary and blocks only dependent work.

## 2. Activation prerequisites

No sprint in this program may become ACTIVE until all are true on `main`:

- GOV-AMD-018 is Accepted;
- GOV-AMD-018 §8 operational reconciliation is complete;
- `governance/execution-profiles/INDEPENDENT-REVIEWER-v1.md` is effective;
- this program transition contract is Founder-merged;
- the individual sprint activation satisfies GOV-AMD-001 Amendment 013 and all applicable RISK-001 requirements.

Proposed governance or branch-local role-hydration language grants no authority.

## 3. Relationship to ASA-OPTIONS-TO-OUTCOMES-2026Q4

`ASA-OPTIONS-TO-OUTCOMES-2026Q4` remains the canonical program for all work already authorized under it, including its existing closure and observation obligations, until that program is explicitly closed or superseded through its own governing process.

This program does **not** silently supersede, nest, transfer, or close that program.

Specifically:

- OUTCOME-INTELLIGENCE-001 remains the canonical owner of the existing outcome ledgers, opportunity identities, AOY history, forward-observation machinery, and its historical closure evidence.
- Any still-pending OI-07 / program-closure observation required by ASA-OPTIONS-TO-OUTCOMES remains an obligation of that program until it closes or is explicitly superseded.
- OUTCOME-VALUE-001 may read and extend the existing canonical ledgers and metrics only through their current owners. It must not create a second outcome ledger, duplicate opportunity identity, or claim closure of ASA-OPTIONS-TO-OUTCOMES.
- If OUTCOME-VALUE discovers a defect in existing OI lineage, persistence, or closure evidence, the defect reopens/reroutes to the canonical existing owner rather than being patched by a parallel implementation.
- Historical observations already captured under ASA-OPTIONS-TO-OUTCOMES retain their original program identity and are not relabeled as if they were produced by this program.

## 4. Remaining old-program duties versus new-program duties

### ASA-OPTIONS-TO-OUTCOMES retains

- completion of its already-authorized pending observation/closure obligations;
- ownership and maintenance of its existing outcome ledger and historical reports;
- correction of defects that invalidate its own prior acceptance claims;
- exact historical interpretation of its own AOY and forward-outcome corpus.

### ASA-PRODUCTION-TO-DECISION owns

- the new production-trust, system-understanding, trade-trust, outcome-value, and scale-readiness acceptance claims;
- new product/operational projections explicitly created by the five sprint prompts;
- extending existing Outcome Intelligence for value analysis without replacing its ledger/identity authority;
- new scale-investment decisions after value evidence is available.

## 5. Cross-program defect routing

If a new sprint exposes a foundational defect in an artifact owned by ASA-OPTIONS-TO-OUTCOMES:

1. identify the canonical owner;
2. reopen or create a bounded correction under that owner/current governance;
3. pause only the new-program path that depends on the invalidated assumption;
4. continue independent authorized work;
5. rerun both the old claim and the new dependent proof after correction.

No duplicate owner is created for convenience.

## 6. Program operating model

- maximum two implementation/correction streams;
- observation streams may remain open without consuming implementation WIP;
- no-lull: CI, review, provider waits, and observation windows do not idle dependency-safe work;
- root-cause fixes target the earliest canonical owner;
- no strategy semantics are changed merely to satisfy product or scale acceptance;
- no paid provider commitment, live broker authority, deployment, destructive action, or material scope expansion occurs without the current Founder-only decision path.

## 7. Review and merge

Each sprint remains independently reviewable and activatable.

The program document does not itself grant:
- merge authority;
- deployment authority;
- role-hydration authority;
- independent-review instantiation authority.

Those come only from accepted governance and an active, Founder-authorized sprint.

Every delegated merge inherits the complete GOV-AMD-001 Amendment 013 floor and applicable RISK-001 gates. Sprint-specific gates are additive, never substitutes.

## 8. Program closure

This program may close only when all five sprint-specific closure criteria are satisfied or explicitly downgraded by the Founder with durable rationale.

Closure of this program does not retroactively close or rewrite ASA-OPTIONS-TO-OUTCOMES. If the older program is still open, its remaining obligations continue independently under its canonical contract.
