# TGSM-RESEARCH-001 — RES-01 evidence qualification

Classification: **DATA_LIMITED**.

The immutable qualification artifact is
`project/research/TGSM-RESEARCH-001/evidence-qualification-v1.json`. It records
the exact implementation baseline, per-requirement evidence status, gaps, and
the conclusions that remain permitted or prohibited.

No result-bearing historical S001 experiment is currently truthful. ASA has no
sealed corpus covering the sector ETFs and SPY with qualified total-return
semantics, no canonical three-month Treasury total-return series, no immutable
historical membership snapshots beyond represented inception boundaries, and
no sealed historical exchange calendar for exceptional closures and historical
rule changes. The existing Alpha Vantage adjusted-history path does not cure
those gaps and its current entitlement limitation remains unresolved.

This is a research-data limitation, not an S001 runtime defect. Production S001
remains frozen. Synthetic fixture work may validate runtime/research decision
equivalence, deterministic identity, serialization, and replay, but may not
produce investment-performance or H1-H5 conclusions.

RES-02 and RES-03 may proceed because preregistration and experiment identity
do not require observing results. RES-04 may prove fixture equivalence. RES-05
through RES-07 must record `INCONCLUSIVE`/not-run for result-bearing claims
unless a later separately authorized evidence source satisfies this artifact's
gaps without weakening semantics.
