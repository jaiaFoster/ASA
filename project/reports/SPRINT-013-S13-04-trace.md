## Conclusion

The canonical value types and the analytics that consume them (findings #1-#3) are **already fully built, tested, and correctly layered** from SPRINT-012 WS2/WS5/WS6 -- this ticket does not need to re-invent them. The entire gap is exactly two things: (a) a durable repository to accumulate `HistoricalSkewObservation`s over time (finding #5), since no provider can supply history truthfully (finding #6), and (b) implementing the two already-Founder-approved policy rules for comparison-universe and sector evidence (findings #7-#8) as real code. Wiring these into `screening/live_adapters.py` (finding #4) is explicitly S13-04D's own scope and is deliberately not touched here.

This trace directly informs the staged PR sequence this ticket's own packet already specifies (S13-04A/B/C/D) -- confirmed as the right sequence, not revised.
